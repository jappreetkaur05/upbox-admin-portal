## 1. Pick confirm or exception (once)

- Each pick stop is **Confirm pick** *or* **Raise exception** — not both.
- Added `PickStop.outcome`: `open` | `picked` | `excepted`.
- After either action, the stop leaves the active “next stop” queue.
- Exception can be raised **once** per line; duplicates blocked.
- Resolve keeps the old stop as history and can append a **new** open stop.
- Sequence badges: Open / Picked / Excepted.

**Files:** `types/outbound.ts`, `outbound.service.ts`, `mockOutbound.ts`, `useOutbound.ts`, `PickingPage.tsx`
